A LITTLE MORE ABOUT THE STUDIO

Good to know.

Who is Mrs. Smash?

Mrs. Smash is Dee, the creator behind JUP LIFE Studio: a mom of four, a wife to one, and grateful for a new chapter of creating.

Where is JUP LIFE Studio based?

JUP LIFE Studio is based in Jupiter, Florida. Everything is designed and 3D-printed here, inspired by the ocean, palm shade and flowers.

What are the coasters made of?

Washable TPU 95A, a flexible, durable material, printed in a single color. Gentle hand wash and air dry; keep away from heat.

Can I buy the coasters now?

Yes. Shop the One More Chapter cradle ($9), Hibiscus Set ($29), Proof of Coin ($39), Jup Signature by Dee ($49) and Sweet Coast Jar ($59). Add your favorites to the bag and pay securely through Stripe. Free U.S. shipping, with dispatch within 2–3 business days.

What’s coming soon?

JUP 100, Jup Bloom, the Happy Place Set and the wedding collection are on their way. They’re not for sale yet, but you can ask Dee about any of them.

Do you make custom logo coasters for restaurants and bars?

Yes. We make custom logo coasters in washable, durable TPU 95A, right here in Jupiter. Tell Dee about your place and your logo, and she’ll follow up personally.

How does the free sample pack work?

Any restaurant or bar in the USA can request a free 6-coaster sample pack in a round holder. Send your details and logo and ask for Dee, Mrs. Smash. We’re especially glad to serve Jupiter, Tequesta, Palm Beach Gardens and Juno Beach.

How do I contact Dee?

Email dee@juplifestudio.com or call 561.532.7120. Follow @juplifestudio on X for studio updates.

What does Est. 10.23.99 mean?

October 23, 1999 is the family’s anniversary and the roots of their story. It is not the studio’s business founding date.
