A LITTLE MORE ABOUT THE STUDIO

Good to know.

Who is Mrs. Smash?

Mrs. Smash is Dee, the creator behind JUP LIFE Studio: a mom of four, a wife to one, and grateful for a new chapter of creating.

Where is JUP LIFE Studio based?

JUP LIFE Studio is based in Jupiter, Florida. Its coastal designs take inspiration from the ocean, palm shade and flowers.

What is in the JUP LIFE collection?

The collection previews six coastal coasters, an open-air holder and the coordinated Happy Place Set. Hibiscus includes the transparent TPU first-print design and an orange PETG V02 prototype with a raised rim, six-coaster case and lid. Jup Bloom remains in development.

Can I buy the coasters now?

The site currently presents collection previews and prototypes. Contact Dee to ask about availability. Final product details, pricing, care and shipping timing are confirmed before sale.

How do I contact Dee?

Email dee@juplifestudio.com or call 561.532.7120. Follow @juplifestudio on X for studio updates.

What does Est. 10.23.99 mean?

October 23, 1999 is the family’s anniversary and the roots of their story. It is not the studio’s business founding date.
