The Tide Dispenser is a refillable, soap-bar-shaped dispenser. A snap-on Tide Skin changes its look whenever the season does.

  • A printed core that holds a standard 28 mm pump bottle
  • Snap-on TPU Tide Skins in coastal colors
  • Hand wash, dish soap and body wash, each marked with a wave icon
  • Care: wipe the core clean and wash it by hand. Do not put the core in the dishwasher.
  • Printed in Jupiter, Florida
