A smoother first print.

Our fine-detail trial starts with a 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.12 mm layers. Use the current profile and drying instructions for your exact orange PETG and printer. Establish reliable flow and consistent surface speed before printing the full set.

PETG remains a firm material. Making a section thinner can increase its flex; it does not give it TPU’s soft feel. The goal here is to compare the feel and finish of two materials while keeping the glass supported.

This V02 changes the rim and moves the art to the top, so it is a design comparison as well as a material comparison. A pure material comparison uses identical geometry in both materials.
