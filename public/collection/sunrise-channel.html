CORAL COVE SERIES / JUP-003

Sunrise Channel.

First light. Weathered rock. A little path to the ocean.

TPU 95A / FIRST PRINT NEXT

A sunrise.
A little detail.

Layered rock shelves frame a widening water channel. A sun above the horizon, little erosion pockets and reflection ripples bring the coast to the table.

Three engraving depths keep the detail recessed below the level support surface. A continuous flat outer band and flat underside complete the design.

  • 104 mm diameter · 4.2 mm overall thickness
  • Recesses at 0.30, 0.55 and 0.75 mm
  • 3.45 mm minimum modeled base thickness
  • TPU 95A target · one material
  • Same outer dimensions as the six-coaster case prototype
