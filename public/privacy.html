THE STUDIO DETAILS

Your privacy.

Effective October 5, 2026. Contact Dee with any questions about your information.

JUP LIFE Studio can receive information you provide when contacting Dee or placing an order, including contact, shipping and order information. Email inquiries use your email provider. Website inquiry forms use Resend to deliver your message and our acknowledgment email. Payments are handled through Stripe’s hosted checkout; this site does not collect your card details.

How information is used

Information supports responding to inquiries, processing orders, delivering products and keeping the studio secure. Stripe processes checkout information under its own privacy terms. Cloudflare hosts the website and stores uploaded studio media and order records. Order records here contain a Stripe session reference, product quantities and payment status. Customer contact and shipping details are managed in Stripe.

Your browser

Your bag is saved locally on your device. Studio administrator sign-in uses a secure session cookie. This build includes no advertising pixels or visitor analytics. Hosting and payment services may process technical information for security and service delivery.

Questions and requests

Contact dee@juplifestudio.com for privacy questions or to request access to or deletion of information, subject to records needed for orders and applicable obligations. We retain information for as long as needed to respond to you, fulfill orders, handle returns and disputes, maintain security and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.
