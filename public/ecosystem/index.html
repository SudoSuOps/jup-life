WHY THIS MODEL WORKS

Use the same infrastructure.
Respect the boundaries.

Shared: printers, compute, materials tracking, maintenance, scheduling and fabrication expertise.

Separated: clinical records, health imagery, permissions, release criteria and sensitive workflows.

Always: human review where it matters, local production when practical, and products designed to stay in use.

THE FACTORY FLOW

From idea
to finished piece.

Every line uses the same basic rhythm, giving the bakery a reusable operating backbone.

  1. 01Intake

    Logo, photo, foot case, event brief or compute job enters the system.

  2. 02Design + compute

    Agents and humans prepare concepts, geometry, previews and production requirements.

  3. 03Quote + route

    Material, machine time, labor, margin and deadlines determine the production path.

  4. 04Make

    Printers, laser, finishing tools and compute execute the work.

  5. 05QA + release

    Nothing ships or gets released until the right checks are complete.

  6. 06Learn + reorder

    Successful jobs become repeatable recipes for faster future production.
