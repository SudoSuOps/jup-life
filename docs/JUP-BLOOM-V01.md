# Jup Bloom V01

Inspired by the founder-supplied floral video, October 3, 2026. Companion to the existing chair-and-wave and hibiscus directions. Palette: petal pink, coral, gold and foliage green. No species attribution is required.

Prototype geometry: 105 mm diameter x 3.6 mm thick; continuous 72 mm central platform. Eight petal outlines recessed 0.6 mm around the perimeter, 1 mm minimum groove width. No raised lettering or ornament under the glass. SVG center lettering is presentation only.

Start single-color TPU 95A on P1S, flat side down, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls and solid fill; no support. Use your established TPU filament profile and direct spool feed. This is a prototype starting point, not validated slicer settings.

Print one first. Verify dimensions, flatness and stability with actual glasses; test wet grip, condensation cleanup and warm-mug response before production. TPU does not provide an absorbent core. No dishwasher, heat-rating or absorbency claims. Existing holder fit must be physically checked.

CAD source: designs/jup-bloom-v01.scad. Printable STL: public/downloads/jup-bloom-v01.stl. Mesh validation: watertight, consistent winding, one connected solid, measured 105 x 105 x 3.6 mm. Physical testing pending.
