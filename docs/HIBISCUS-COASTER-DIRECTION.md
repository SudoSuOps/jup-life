# Hibiscus coaster — design direction

Source: public/assets/hibiscus-inspiration.webp, supplied by the founder on October 3, 2026.

Use the large foreground hibiscus as the focal motif; palms and blue sky establish the supporting palette. Keep the flower recognizable with five broad petals, a deep berry throat and a small stamen detail. Preserve JUP LIFE's chair-and-wave identity as a companion collection.

Prototype proposal (not released specifications): 100 mm round coaster, 4 mm overall thickness, flat drink-contact surface and rounded perimeter. Start with the existing planned TPU 95A material. Keep decorative relief below the drink-contact plane; avoid raised stamens that could rock a glass. Explore a pink/berry face on a palm-green base and a sky-blue alternative. Use a single-color embossed version if color changes are impractical. Put small JUP LIFE branding on the underside.

Before release: confirm actual dimensions, flatness, glass stability, wet grip, condensation drainage, cleanability and heat response using printed samples. Do not advertise dishwasher safety or heat ratings without validation. This brief is inspiration for a build, not a validated manufacturing file.
